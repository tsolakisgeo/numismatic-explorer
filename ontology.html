Data architecture

Ontology-first, interface-ready.

The Turtle records are authoritative. The website generates a disposable JSON search index without flattening away the original URIs.

Primary resource

Every physical coin is represented as nmo:NumismaticObject. Identifiers, dates, measurements, authorities, mints, materials, denominations, collections, findspots, faces, legends, control marks, and type-series links are read from RDF properties.

nmo:hasAuthorityIssuing authority
nmo:hasMintPlace of manufacture or issue
nmo:hasMaterialMaterial URI
nmo:hasDenominationDenominational assignment
nmo:hasObverseObverse face resource
nmo:hasReverseReverse face resource
nmo:hasTypeSeriesItemExternal typological attribution
nmo:hasFindspotFindspot resource

Archival evidence

Inventory cards remain separate cor:InventoryCard resources connected to the physical coin. Their transcriptions, raw readings, database context, and uncertainty notes remain available in the record data, while card images are intentionally not displayed in this release.

Photograph mapping

data/coin_photos.csv is a lightweight many-to-one mapping. Repeating a coin identifier creates multiple photograph-resource links. Two-column tab-separated rows are accepted, and optional columns can later provide side labels or direct image URLs.
